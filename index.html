How the plan works

Eat around your daily target. A weekly average matters more than any single day — one big day doesn't ruin it.

Hit your protein first. It keeps you full and protects muscle while you lose fat. Eggs, chicken, fish, beans, moi moi, suya, Greek yoghurt.

Watch the swallow and the oil. A big wrap of eba or pounded yam with oily soup can be 800+ kcal. Smaller wrap, more protein in the soup, less palm oil.

Drinks count. Malt, soft drinks and juice add up fast — swap for water, zero-sugar drinks, or unsweetened tea.

Walk. 8–10k steps a day makes the deficit much easier. Add 2–3 strength sessions a week if you can.

Weight jumps around 1–2 kg day to day (water, salt). Judge progress on the trend over 2–3 weeks.

Every 5 kg lost, update your weight — the target recalculates automatically.

Calories for Nigerian dishes are rough averages — portions and oil vary a lot. If you have a health condition or feel dizzy or unwell, check with a doctor.
